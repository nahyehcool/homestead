About Homestead

What is Homestead Support?

We're a small Melbourne business. Your cook comes to your home, or your parent's, and cooks the food you love in your own kitchen, the way you like it.

Is this a meal delivery service?

No. Your cook makes fresh meals in your kitchen, stores the extras and tidies up. It can sit alongside meal delivery if you already use one.

Who is Homestead for?

Older Australians who want to keep enjoying home cooking from their own culture, in their own kitchen, and the families who help arrange it.

Where do you work?

Melbourne's north-east, in Banyule and Nillumbik, including Greensborough and Eltham. Somewhere else? Tell us your suburb. We can't promise, but we'd love to know where families need us.

Food, shopping and meals

Which cuisines do you cook?

Right now: Modern Australian, Italian, French, Greek and Korean. If your family's food isn't on the list, tell us what they love and we'll talk it through honestly.

Can the cook make my parent's own recipes?

Yes, if they'd like. Your parent chooses the dishes and can show the cook how they like them, or just describe the taste they remember.

Can you cook for special diets?

We can cook vegetarian, salt-reduced and gluten-free dishes. We currently can't cook for serious or life-threatening allergies, coeliac-level gluten-free diets, medical diets, or texture and swallowing needs. Tell us what your parent likes, and we'll talk it through at the meet and greet.

Who buys the groceries?

You choose and buy the groceries, and we'll send you a shopping list to make it easy. That way, the $200 is all you pay us.

What about the meals in the fridge?

Fridge meals are best eaten within about 3 days, and the rest can go in the freezer. Your cook leaves a note on how to store them.

Can you help organise the fridge, freezer and pantry?

Not in the Launch Offer, but you can ask for it later. It needs planning ahead, so mention it when you book or ask for a call back.

Our cooks, cost and contracts

How are your cooks checked?

Every Homestead cook is experienced in the kitchen, with food safety training and a current NDIS Worker Screening Check.

What happens after the Launch Offer, and what does it cost?

Just tell us if you'd like to continue. We'll talk about what suits your parent and price it to match.

Is there a contract? Do I need to cancel?

No. There's no lock-in contract, so there's nothing to cancel.

Can I use Support at Home, CHSP or NDIS funding, and do we need an aged care package?

Not yet. We're a private service, so we can't currently bill Support at Home, CHSP or NDIS budgets. That also means you don't need an aged care package or assessment to book.

To explore government support, start with My Aged Care on 1800 200 422 or myagedcare.gov.au.
