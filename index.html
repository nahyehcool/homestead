Meet your cook

Hi, I'm Inyoung. I'll be the one in your kitchen.

I've spent seven years cooking in professional kitchens, including as Sous Chef at Gilbert Street Hotel. I'm a qualified chef, and cooking for people is still my favourite thing to do.

A while ago I went home to South Korea to see my parents, and joined them for Kimjang. That's when the family gets together to make a winter's worth of kimchi. Mum and Dad are in their 80s now. They love their home and they love their food, but doing it all themselves takes a lot more out of them these days. Mum told me this might be their last Kimjang.

That stayed with me. I wanted them to keep enjoying the food they've always loved, right there in their own kitchen. And I knew lots of families here would want the same for their mum or dad.

So I started Homestead Support. I'm also a service designer, which really just means I listen first. Then I keep making things better based on what each family tells me.

Our Launch Offer

How your Launch Offer works.

We're just starting out, so our first 10 households get the Launch Offer for $200.

  1. We phone you

    Book, or ask for a call back, and we'll phone you to talk it through.

  2. Complimentary meet and greet, at home or by video

    We start with a friendly chat about what your mum or dad likes to eat, and what would make cooking easier. Meet by video and show us the kitchen if you like, or we can visit. Nothing booked, nothing owed.

  3. Choose the menu

    We send a few menus, the way you like to be contacted. You and your parent pick a favourite.

  4. Sort the groceries

    You buy the groceries from our shopping list, so $200 is all you pay us.

  5. Cooking day

    Your cook comes on a weekday for 2 hours, cooks the way you like it, and tidies up.

  6. Menu and number of meals

    Between 2 and 8 meals, depending on the menu. Store them or share them, with a note on how to keep them.

  7. Your feedback

    Tell us what worked, what didn't and what you'd change. It helps us get this right for you and every family after you.

Before you book

What we do, and what we don't.

We cook

We cook meals in your home with groceries you choose and buy.

We're not a registered aged care provider, nursing service or dietitian. We don't help with showering, dressing, toileting, moving around or medications, and we don't give medical or nutrition advice.

We currently can't cook for medical diets or swallowing needs. For that kind of support, your parent's GP or aged care provider is the best place to start.

Allergies and dietary needs

Cooking safely around allergies takes both of us.

What you do: Tell us about every allergy, intolerance and texture need before the first visit, and if anything changes. You buy the groceries, so please check labels against your parent's list.

What we do: We ask again at every visit, read labels on what's in the kitchen, and stop and tell you if something doesn't look right.

What we can't promise: A home kitchen can't be made allergen-free. We currently can't cook for serious or life-threatening allergies, such as one that has caused anaphylaxis. Unsure? Email Inyoung before you book at our team.
