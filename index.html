Our Launch Offer

How your Launch Offer works.

We're just starting out, so our first 10 households get a special Launch Offer.

  1. We phone you

    Book, or ask for a call back, and we'll phone you to talk it through.

  2. Complimentary meet and greet, at home or by video

    We start with a friendly chat about what your mum or dad likes to eat, and what would make cooking easier. Meet by video and show us the kitchen if you like, or we can visit. Nothing booked, nothing owed.

  3. Choose the menu

    Tell us what you'd like us to cook, or we'll send a few menus, the way you like to be contacted. You and your parent pick a favourite.

  4. Groceries delivered

    We prepare your Woolworths order for the menu. You approve it, pay at checkout, and it's delivered before cooking day.

  5. Cooking day

    Your cook comes for 2 hours and cooks the way your family likes, with you guiding as much or as little as you like. Then we tidy up.

  6. Your feedback

    Tell us what worked, what didn't and what you'd change. It helps us get this right for you and every family after you.

Meet your cook

Hi, I'm Inyoung. I'll be the one in your kitchen.

I've spent seven years cooking in professional kitchens, including as Sous Chef at Gilbert Street Hotel. I'm a qualified chef, and cooking for people is still my favourite thing to do.

A while ago I went home to South Korea to see my parents, and joined them for Kimjang. Read more

That's when the family gets together to make a winter's worth of kimchi. Mum and Dad are in their 80s now. They love their home and they love their food, but doing it all themselves takes a lot more out of them these days. Mum told me this might be their last Kimjang.

That stayed with me. I wanted them to keep enjoying the food they've always loved, right there in their own kitchen. And I knew lots of families here would want the same for their mum or dad.

So I started Homestead Support. I'm also a service designer, which really just means I listen first. Then I keep making things better based on what each family tells me. Read less
