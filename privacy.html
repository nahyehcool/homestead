Privacy Notice

Last updated 11 November 2026

In short

  • We collect only what we need to arrange and cook your meals.
  • We do not collect health information.
  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete it any time.

Homestead Support is run by Inyoung Choi, trading as Homestead Support, ABN 39 856 096 960, Victoria, Australia. This notice explains what personal information we collect, why, and what we do with it. When we say "we", we mean Inyoung Choi.

What we collect

  • Your contact details: your name, email address, phone number and suburb, which we use to reply to your request and to phone you.
  • What you tell us in our forms: who the Launch Offer is for, the food your parent loves and the language they speak, your best time to call, and anything else you choose to write.
  • Cooking address: the address where we will cook, which you give us separately when you book. We use it only to arrange and attend the visit.
  • Your parent's food preferences: their name, the foods they like, cultural or religious food practices, and any minor allergies, which we use only to choose what to cook. We do not collect health information.
  • Booking and payment records: what was booked, when, and what was paid. We use Xero to send invoices and take card payments, so your payment details are handled by Xero. We do not see or keep full card numbers.
  • Messages: emails or calls you send us.

We collect this directly from you. If you are booking for a parent, we may also ask your parent directly, and we will ask you to confirm that they know you are sharing their food preferences with us.

Why we collect it

We use your information to plan and cook meals that suit your parent's preferences, to arrange visits, to send invoices and keep our business records, to contact you about a booking, and to meet our legal obligations. We only use it for those purposes, or for a closely related one you would reasonably expect.

Dietary preferences and allergies

We ask for food preferences and minor allergies only so we can choose what to cook. We do not collect health information, and we do not currently serve clients with serious or life-threatening allergies, intolerances, texture or swallowing needs, or medical diets. If your parent has any of these, we are not able to cook for them at this time. Only your cook sees what you tell us. We do not use it for marketing and we do not sell it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only:

  • with the services that help us run Homestead, such as Xero for invoicing and card payments, and our website and email providers, and only what they need to do their job;
  • in an emergency, for example calling an ambulance and passing on the contact details we hold;
  • with someone you ask us to share it with, or have agreed to; and
  • where the law requires it.

How we keep it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as we need it to provide the service and to meet our business and tax record requirements, then we delete it securely. If a data breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you or your parent, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are able to. Email us and we will reply as soon as we reasonably can. If your parent is the person the information is about, they have these rights too.

Questions and complaints

Please contact us first and we will try to fix it quickly. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to:

  • the Health Complaints Commissioner (Victoria), about how we handle dietary and allergy information; or
  • the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), if the Privacy Act 1988 applies to us.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page when what we do changes, and show the date at the top.
